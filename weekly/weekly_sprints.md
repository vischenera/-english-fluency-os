# Weekly Sprints

## Weekly Card Budget

- 15 frameworks or expressions,
- 10 vocabulary words,
- 5 corrections,
- 2 pronunciation targets.

## Weekly Review Day

Record a 60-second answer to one prompt:

1. What did you work on this week?
2. What problem did you solve recently?
3. What is one decision you need to make?
4. What is one thing you are learning?

Score using `metrics.md`.

## Progress Rule

A week is successful if speaking feels 5% easier, not if every card is perfect.
