# Conversation Islands

About 80% of everyday and work conversation lands on the same few topics. Instead of inventing answers live, you prepare a small set of 60-second "islands" — short, reusable monologues about your own life. You refine each one over weeks until it comes out smoothly. In real conversations you then *jump between islands* you already own, which removes most freezing.

## How To Build An Island

1. Pick one topic below.
2. Speak for 60 seconds. Record it.
3. Keep the 5–8 best sentences. Drop the rest.
4. Re-tell it the next day, a little smoother.
5. An island is "done" when you can tell it with almost no pauses — then keep it warm with occasional retelling.

Use frameworks (`I'm working on…`, `The reason is…`, `These days…`) and chunks (`to be honest`, `at the end of the day`) inside your islands.

## The Core 6 Islands

### Island 1 — What I do
Your role, your team, what a normal day looks like, what you're responsible for.
- Starters: *I work as… / My main job is to… / On a normal day, I…*

### Island 2 — What I'm working on now
Your current project or main task, why it matters, where it stands.
- Starters: *Right now I'm working on… / The goal is to… / We're at the stage where…*

### Island 3 — Where I'm from
Your hometown, what it's like, what you like about it, how it compares to where you are now.
- Starters: *I'm originally from… / It's a place where… / The thing I miss most is…*

### Island 4 — My weekend / free time
What you usually do, a recent example, what you enjoy about it.
- Starters: *Most weekends I… / Last weekend I… / I'm really into…*

### Island 5 — Something I'm learning or improving
A skill, a habit, why you picked it, how it's going.
- Starters: *These days I'm trying to… / The reason I started is… / So far it's…*

### Island 6 — A recent problem I handled
A small work or life problem, what you did, how it turned out. (Great for interviews and updates.)
- Starters: *Recently I ran into… / What I did was… / In the end…*

## Optional Extra Islands (add later)

- A decision you're weighing right now.
- Your opinion on a change at work.
- A goal for the next few months.
