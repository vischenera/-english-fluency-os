# Expression Library

These are conversation shortcuts — ready phrases whose social job is already packaged, so you don't build them word by word. A key group is **bridges**: phrases that buy you a second so you keep talking instead of pausing silently. Learn one in under 60 seconds and use it in a real reply.

### Bridges (buy time)

## E001. That's a good question.
**Tag:** bridge · **Formality:** neutral
**Use:** Someone asks you something you weren't ready for and you need a moment before answering.
**Drill:** Say it, then begin your real answer without pausing.

## E002. Let me think for a second.
**Tag:** bridge · **Formality:** neutral
**Use:** You openly take a beat to gather your thoughts instead of going silent.
**Drill:** Say it, count one breath, then start your point.

## E003. How should I put this?
**Tag:** bridge · **Formality:** neutral
**Use:** You're searching for the right words to phrase something delicate or complex.
**Drill:** Say it, then give your first attempt at the wording.

## E004. The way I see it…
**Tag:** bridge · **Formality:** neutral
**Use:** You signal an opinion is coming and ease into stating your view.
**Drill:** Say it, then finish with one clear opinion.

## E005. What I'm trying to say is…
**Tag:** bridge · **Formality:** neutral
**Use:** You've rambled a bit and want to land on your main point cleanly.
**Drill:** Say it, then state your point in one sentence.

## E006. Let me put it this way.
**Tag:** bridge · **Formality:** neutral
**Use:** Your first explanation didn't land, so you reframe it more simply.
**Drill:** Say it, then give a simpler version of your idea.

## E007. Off the top of my head…
**Tag:** bridge · **Formality:** casual
**Use:** You give a quick, unprepared answer without committing to exact details.
**Drill:** Say it, then offer one rough answer or number.

## E008. Good question — let me think.
**Tag:** bridge · **Formality:** neutral
**Use:** You acknowledge a tough question and buy time in the same breath.
**Drill:** Say it, then start answering after a short pause.
