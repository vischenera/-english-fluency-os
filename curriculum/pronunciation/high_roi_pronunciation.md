# High-ROI Pronunciation

Goal: clear international English, not native accent elimination. Practice one tiny drill per day.

## P001. TH /θ/ in think

**Card:** Place tongue lightly between teeth and blow air: think, thanks, three.
**Explanation:** This sound is often replaced with t/s/d. Clarity improves many common words.
**Examples:** think, thank, three, thirty, method.
**Quick drill:** think–sink, thin–tin, three–tree.
**Malayalam-speaker notes:** Do not press the tongue hard like dental /t/. Keep airflow continuous.
**ROI Score:** 10/10
**Reason it survived:** Very common sound with high listener impact.

## P002. TH /ð/ in this

**Card:** Tongue lightly between teeth, voice on: this, that, these.
**Explanation:** Voiced th appears in extremely common grammar words.
**Examples:** this, that, these, those, mother.
**Quick drill:** this–dis, they–day, then–den.
**Malayalam-speaker notes:** Keep it soft; avoid a strong d.
**ROI Score:** 10/10
**Reason it survived:** Mispronouncing this sound affects very frequent words.

## P003. V vs W

**Card:** V uses teeth + lip; W uses rounded lips only.
**Explanation:** Mixing v/w can change meaning and reduce clarity.
**Examples:** very/wary, vine/wine, vest/west, vet/wet.
**Quick drill:** very well, we value work, west village.
**Malayalam-speaker notes:** For v, touch upper teeth to lower lip. For w, do not touch teeth.
**ROI Score:** 10/10
**Reason it survived:** Common Malayalam-transfer issue with many professional words.

## P004. Clear R

**Card:** Curl or bunch the tongue slightly back; do not roll strongly.
**Explanation:** International English r should be clear but not over-trilled.
**Examples:** right, report, priority, improve, project.
**Quick drill:** right report, real reason, priority project.
**Malayalam-speaker notes:** Avoid a strong Malayalam-style tap/roll when speaking English.
**ROI Score:** 9/10
**Reason it survived:** R appears in high-frequency work words and affects clarity.

## P005. Word Stress

**Card:** Make one syllable louder/longer: IM-pact noun, im-PACT verb.
**Explanation:** Wrong stress can make known words hard to understand.
**Examples:** PRE-sent vs pre-SENT, RE-cord vs re-CORD, DE-tail, im-POR-tant.
**Quick drill:** Say each word once flat, then once with strong stress.
**Malayalam-speaker notes:** Malayalam rhythm can make syllables too equal; English needs a clear stress peak.
**ROI Score:** 10/10
**Reason it survived:** Stress improves clarity across thousands of words without learning new vocabulary.

## Minimal 60-Second Drill

1. Pick one row.
2. Say 5 words slowly.
3. Say 2 minimal pairs.
4. Use one word in a sentence.
5. Stop before fatigue.
