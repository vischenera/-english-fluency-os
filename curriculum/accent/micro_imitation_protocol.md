# Micro-Imitation Protocol (ADHD-First)

Traditional shadowing — sitting through a TED talk and repeating it — does not survive an ADHD brain or boredom. This replaces it with 5–30 second hits riding on content you already like or sentences you actually use. No long audio, ever.

## The 5-Mode Menu — pick ONE by mood each day

You don't follow a fixed routine. Each day, glance at the menu and pick whatever you can stand right now. Novelty is the point.

| Mode | What you do | Time | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Stress-tap | Take any sentence, tap the strong beats while you say it (prosody cards) | 20s | nothing |
| 2. Minimal pair | One segmental card — say the contrast pair, feel the mouth | 30s | nothing |
| 3. Echo-a-show | Pause something you're watching, repeat the last line in their voice | 30s | a show you like |
| 4. TTS-your-line | Type a sentence YOU want to say into US text-to-speech, mimic it 3× | 1 min | phone/browser |
| 5. Record-compare | Record one sentence, play a model (YouGlish/TTS), fix one thing, re-record | 2 min | phone |

Most days = Mode 1, 2, or 3. Modes 4–5 on review days or when you have energy.

## Mode 3 — Echo-a-show (the best ADHD fit)

While watching anything with General American speakers (a show, a YouTuber, a movie) — **the entertainment carries your attention, you smuggle in a rep:**
1. When a line lands, pause.
2. Say it once, copying the rhythm and tone, not just the words.
3. Unpause. That's it.
- One good line a day beats a forced 20-minute session.
- Mimic the *melody* (up/down, which word is loudest), not only the sounds.

## Mode 4 — TTS-your-own-line (zero hunting)

The lowest-friction option, and it doubles as fluency practice because you use *your* content:
1. Type a sentence from your conversation islands or something you'll actually say.
2. Play it in a US text-to-speech voice.
3. Say it with the voice 3×, then once on your own.
- This fuses accent + fluency on material you care about — no searching for "interesting" audio.

## Mode 5 — Record-compare (the feedback engine)

Once or twice a week:
1. Record yourself saying one sentence.
2. Play a model: the same words on YouGlish (US) or TTS.
3. Find **one** difference (a beat in the wrong place, a flat vowel, a missing flap-T).
4. Fix that one thing and re-record. Done.
- One fix per session. Don't chase everything.

## Scoring & streak

End each micro-session with a 0–3 tap:
- 0 = skipped, 1 = did it, 2 = felt one improvement, 3 = nailed it.
Track the streak, not perfection. The win condition is *showing up daily*, because accent change is cumulative reps, not big sessions.

## How it plugs into the daily loop

Minute 9 of `daily/10_minute_protocol.md` is your accent slot — just run one menu mode. That's enough to compound over weeks. Add a second mode anytime you've got the spark; never force it.
