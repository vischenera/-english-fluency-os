# Roadmaps

All roadmaps assume **10 minutes per day**. If you miss a day, do not compensate with a long session. Resume the next day. New cards flow through the spaced-repetition ladder in `reviews/review_protocol.md`.

## Core Weekly Structure

- Days 1–5: learn and retrieve new cards (within the weekly budget).
- Day 6: mixed speaking practice + retell one conversation island.
- Day 7: record 60 seconds and score (including longest fluent run).
- Every day, minute 9: one accent menu mode — start with prosody (rhythm) from week 1, since it carries the most accent weight.

## 8-Week Sprint — Minimum Effective Fluency

| Week | Focus | Daily Load | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Core starters + confidence | 3 frameworks, 2 chunks, 1 correction | Can begin answers without freezing. |
| 2 | Opinions + reasons | 3 frameworks, 2 chunks, 1 correction | Can explain simple opinions with because/so/however. |
| 3 | Work & planning language | 3 frameworks, 2 chunks, 2 expressions | Can discuss tasks, timelines, priorities. |
| 4 | Clarifying, asking & bridges | 2 frameworks, 4 expressions, 1 correction | Can ask for context, repeat, confirm, and recover without freezing. |
| 5 | Story & experience + islands | 3 frameworks, 2 chunks, build 2 islands | Can describe past events with fewer tense errors. |
| 6 | Agreement / disagreement | 2 frameworks, 4 expressions, 1 correction | Can participate in discussions politely. |
| 7 | Accent: rhythm & key sounds | 2 frameworks, prosody cards + 2 segmental tells | Stronger GenAm rhythm; the top sounds less marked. |
| 8 | Integration | mixed review + island retells | Can speak for 2 minutes on common topics with a measurably longer fluent run. |

## 12-Week Build — Professional Conversation Readiness

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Expected Outcome |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Foundation | 1–4 | starters, chunks, corrections | Short answers become faster and cleaner. |
| Expansion | 5–8 | planning, meetings, decisions, islands | Can handle work updates and problem-solving talk. |
| Control | 9–12 | nuance, repair, bridges, pronunciation | Can clarify, disagree, summarize, and recover smoothly. |

## 24-Week Maintenance — 90–95% Practical Fluency Target

| Phase | Weeks | Goal | Expected Outcome |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Activation | 1–4 | make known English speakable | Faster sentence starts. |
| Stabilization | 5–8 | reduce common errors | More accurate everyday speech. |
| Professional Range | 9–12 | meetings, planning, decisions | Stronger workplace participation. |
| Spontaneity | 13–16 | random prompts and stories | Less freezing under pressure. |
| Clarity | 17–20 | pronunciation and stress | Easier listening comprehension for others. |
| Autonomy | 21–24 | self-directed speaking loops | Learner can maintain fluency without course dependency. |

The core deck is small (~300 cards), but at the weekly card budget a full pass takes about 18 weeks — the 90 corrections are the longest deck. On the 8- and 12-week paths, prioritize the cards you need most (the corrections you actually make, per the review loop) and spend spare time *repeating* high-value cards — which is where fluency is actually built.

## Pareto Rule For Skipping

Skip an item only if you can already use it instantly in speech three times in a row with no hesitation. Recognition is not enough.
