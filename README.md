# English Fluency OS

A Pareto-optimized speaking system for a Malayalam-speaking learner who already understands English and wants maximum practical fluency improvement from **10 minutes per day**.

This is not a traditional English course. It treats fluency as a **retrieval-speed problem**: you already know many words and rules, but conversation fails when useful sentence patterns do not appear fast enough.

## Design Principles

- **Smallest effective curriculum:** keep only items with high conversational ROI.
- **One concept per card:** every card is designed to take under 60 seconds.
- **Retrieval before knowledge:** speak first, then correct.
- **Visible progress:** daily scores, weekly recordings, and stage outcomes.
- **ADHD-friendly:** short loops, no long lectures, no giant vocabulary lists in one session.
- **International clarity:** clear pronunciation, not native accent imitation.

## The 10-Minute Daily Loop

1. **1 min — Warm start:** say yesterday’s best 3 expressions.
2. **3 min — Framework retrieval:** use 3 frameworks in new sentences.
3. **2 min — Correction pattern:** fix one recurring mistake aloud.
4. **2 min — Expression activation:** answer 2 real-life prompts.
5. **1 min — Pronunciation micro-drill:** one sound or stress target.
6. **1 min — Score:** mark retrieval speed, hesitation, and confidence.

## Success Definition

By completing the core system, the learner should be able to:

- start and maintain everyday conversations,
- explain opinions and decisions,
- participate in professional discussions,
- reduce Malayalam-transfer grammar mistakes,
- speak with fewer pauses,
- sound clear enough for international communication.

## What This System Removes

- long grammar chapters,
- rare vocabulary,
- passive listening marathons,
- accent perfection,
- memorizing isolated word lists,
- exercises that do not transfer to speaking.

## How To Use

Start with `daily/10_minute_protocol.md`, follow the 8-week roadmap first, and use `metrics.md` every week. Do not study more cards because motivation is high. The system works through **consistent short retrieval**, not heroic long sessions.
