# Roadmaps

All roadmaps assume **10 minutes per day**. If you miss a day, do not compensate with a long session. Resume the next day.

## Core Weekly Structure

- Days 1–5: learn and retrieve new cards.
- Day 6: mixed speaking practice.
- Day 7: record 60 seconds and score.

## 8-Week Sprint — Minimum Effective Fluency

| Week | Focus | Daily Load | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Core starters + confidence | 3 frameworks, 2 expressions, 1 correction | Can begin answers without freezing. |
| 2 | Opinions + reasons | 3 frameworks, 2 words, 1 correction | Can explain simple opinions with because/so/however. |
| 3 | Work and planning language | 3 frameworks, 3 words, 2 expressions | Can discuss tasks, timelines, priorities. |
| 4 | Clarifying and asking | 2 frameworks, 3 expressions, 1 correction | Can ask for context, repeat, confirm, and recover. |
| 5 | Story and experience | 3 frameworks, 2 words, 1 correction | Can describe past events with fewer tense errors. |
| 6 | Agreement/disagreement | 2 frameworks, 4 expressions, 1 correction | Can participate in discussions politely. |
| 7 | Pronunciation clarity | 2 frameworks, 1 sound drill, 1 stress drill | Better clarity on th, v/w, r, and stress. |
| 8 | Integration | mixed review only | Can speak for 2 minutes on common topics with measurable hesitation reduction. |

## 12-Week Build — Professional Conversation Readiness

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Expected Outcome |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Foundation | 1–4 | starters, reasons, corrections | Short answers become faster and cleaner. |
| Expansion | 5–8 | planning, meetings, decisions | Can handle work updates and problem-solving talk. |
| Control | 9–12 | nuance, repair, pronunciation | Can clarify, disagree, summarize, and recover smoothly. |

## 24-Week Maintenance — 90–95% Practical Fluency Target

| Phase | Weeks | Goal | Expected Outcome |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Activation | 1–4 | make known English speakable | Faster sentence starts. |
| Stabilization | 5–8 | reduce common errors | More accurate everyday speech. |
| Professional Range | 9–12 | meetings, planning, decisions | Stronger workplace participation. |
| Spontaneity | 13–16 | random prompts and stories | Less freezing under pressure. |
| Clarity | 17–20 | pronunciation and stress | Easier international listening comprehension for others. |
| Autonomy | 21–24 | self-directed speaking loops | Learner can maintain fluency without course dependency. |

## Pareto Rule For Skipping

Skip an item only if you can already use it instantly in speech three times in a row with no hesitation. Recognition is not enough.
