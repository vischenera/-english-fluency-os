# Accent Track — Overview (General American)

**Goal:** sound *minimally-marked* in General American — the point where listeners stop noticing your accent. "Completely undetectable" is the north star; "they stop placing you" is the realistic, achievable deliverable.

**Why this is doable without long study or boring audio:** an accent is judged from a *small* set of features, not every sound. Hit those and most of the perceived gap closes. And the biggest lever — rhythm — needs almost no audio at all.

## The Pareto order (do it in this sequence)

1. **Prosody** (`01_prosody.md`) — rhythm, stress, weak forms, intonation. **Biggest impact, mostly no audio.** Start here.
2. **The segmental tells** (`02_segmental_tells.md`) — the ~10 individual sounds that most flag a Malayalam/Indian accent (retroflex t/d, aspiration, v/w, th, rhotic r, diphthongs…).
3. **Connected speech** (`03_connected_speech.md`) — blending words so you sound smooth, not word-by-word (flap-T, gonna/wanna, linking).

Plus: the **fluency layer** (chunks, islands, bridges) is the *other half* of sounding native — a slight accent with native rhythm and phrasing reads as far more native than perfect sounds delivered haltingly.

## How audio works (no files, no long clips)

You never sit through a podcast. When a card says "hear a model," you pull a **single word or line** on demand:
- **YouGlish** (set accent: US) — real native clips of any word/phrase in context.
- **Forvo** — single-word native pronunciations.
- **US text-to-speech** — type your *own* sentence, hear it instantly.

Then **record yourself and compare.** That record-compare loop is the feedback engine.

## ADHD ground rules

- Every card is a **20–40 second micro-game**. You never need sustained focus.
- **Pick by mood** from the 5-mode menu (see `micro_imitation_protocol.md`) — novelty is the fuel.
- Shadow **content you already enjoy** or **your own sentences via TTS** — never assigned audio.
- One tiny action, score it, done. Streak beats intensity.

## Honest limits

At a few minutes a day you'll get clearly less-marked and smoother. To approach *undetectable* you'll eventually want more daily imitation volume and, ideally, a periodic outside check (a pronunciation app that scores you, or a coach) — because your own ear is the last bottleneck. That's a later phase, not a blocker now.
