# Weekly Sprints

## Weekly Card Budget

Keep it small so cards actually graduate through the spaced-repetition ladder:

- 10 frameworks,
- 8 chunks,
- 8 expressions,
- 5 corrections,
- 1–2 pronunciation targets,
- keep 1 conversation island warm.

Do not add new cards while Box 1 (daily review) is full.

## Weekly Review Day

Record a 60-second answer to one prompt (see `daily/prompt_bank.md`), for example:

1. What did you work on this week?
2. What problem did you solve recently?
3. What is one decision you need to make?
4. What is one thing you are learning?

Score using `metrics.md`. Note your **longest fluent run** so it can grow week to week.

## Progress Rule

A week is successful if speaking feels about 5% easier, not if every card is perfect.
