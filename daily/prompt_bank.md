# Prompt Bank

The daily protocol's "expression activation" step needs something to answer. Pick prompts from here so you never waste time deciding what to talk about. Answer aloud in 2–4 sentences, using at least one framework, chunk, or expression you studied today.

Rotate through them; don't answer the same one two days in a row. Reuse a prompt every couple of weeks to feel the improvement.

## Everyday

1. What did you do today?
2. What are your plans for tomorrow?
3. Describe your morning routine.
4. What did you eat today, and was it good?
5. What's the weather like, and how does it affect your day?
6. Tell me about your last weekend.
7. What's something small that made you happy recently?
8. What's something that annoyed you this week?
9. Describe the place where you live.
10. What do you usually do to relax?

## Opinions & decisions

11. What's a decision you need to make soon?
12. Do you prefer working from home or the office? Why?
13. What's one thing you would change about your daily schedule?
14. What's a recent purchase you think was worth it?
15. Is it better to plan everything or stay flexible? Why?
16. What's an opinion you hold that others disagree with?
17. What would you do with one extra free hour each day?

## Work & professional

18. What did you work on this week?
19. What problem did you solve recently, and how?
20. Explain your current project to someone outside your team.
21. What's blocking you right now?
22. Describe a task you're responsible for.
23. What's the next step on something you're working on?
24. How would you give a status update for this week?
25. Describe a time you disagreed with a colleague politely.
26. What's a priority for you this month?
27. Explain a process you do regularly, step by step.

## Stories & experience

28. Tell me about a trip you took.
29. Describe a mistake you made and what you learned.
30. Talk about a person who influenced you.
31. Describe something new you tried recently.
32. Tell me about a time something didn't go as planned.
33. What's a skill you're proud of, and how did you learn it?

## Hypothetical & spontaneous (harder)

34. If you could improve one thing about your city, what would it be?
35. Explain how to make your favourite dish.
36. If a friend visited for a day, what would you show them?
37. Describe your ideal weekend.
38. What advice would you give your younger self?
39. Convince me to try a hobby you enjoy.
40. What do you think you'll be working on a year from now?
