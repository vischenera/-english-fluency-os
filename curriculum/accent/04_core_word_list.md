# Accent Track 4 — Core Word List

The minimum set of words to drill each accent sound on, picked from data: for each sound, the 8 words in which you say it most often in everyday English. **Covers** is the share of that sound, across everything you say, that those 8 words account for.

## Start with these 25

**the, to, and, of, that, with, have, for, this, was, will, are, don't, were, their, from, there, time, after, over, people, we, or, your, so**

These are the words where your accent shows most often, counting every sound below. Getting them right fixes about 22% of all your accent slips (each time a sound comes out non-American), three-quarters of what the whole 81-word list fixes. Say each one slowly with every sound right, checking its rows below, then use it in a short phrase.

## How to use it (60 seconds)

1. Pick one row a day: today's segmental card's row, or any row by mood.
2. Say the words slowly once, feeling the mouth position from the card, then once at normal speed.
3. Say the chunk 3×, linking the words.
4. Optional: play one word on YouGlish (US) or a US text-to-speech and compare.

## Why not 80%?

A few sounds live in a handful of words: 8 words cover 88% of the voiced TH. Most are spread across the whole language: 80% of every R you say takes about 2,200 different words, and 80% of all twelve sounds over 3,000, nearly every word you know. So the rest comes from the mouth habit: build it on these rows, and once it's automatic it carries to every other word. Returns flatten fast too: on average, 5 words per sound cover 27%, 8 cover 32% (this list), 20 cover 41%, and 50 only 50%.

Four spread-out sounds make most of the slips: R (21% of all slips), final consonants (13%), T/D (12%) and the P/T/K puff (10%). Their habit drills (SG06, SG10, SG01, SG02) are the best use of any extra time.
